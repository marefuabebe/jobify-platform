Freelancer Help

Freelancer Success FAQ

Everything you need to know about proposals, escrow payouts, and building a high-income freelance career.

How do I stand out and land my first project on Jobify?

Complete your profile to 100%, upload past portfolio pieces or GitHub links, and write concise, problem-focused proposals that directly reference the client's project description. Completing identity verification also adds the Verified Pro badge to your bids.

When and how do I receive payments for completed work?

For fixed-price projects, funds are released immediately upon client milestone approval. For hourly work, tracked hours are billed weekly. You can withdraw available funds anytime to your bank account, PayPal, or Wise.

How do I earn the Verified Pro badge?

The Verified Pro badge is awarded to freelancers who submit government ID verification, link verified work credentials or code repositories, and maintain a stellar track record of high-rated milestone completions.

How does Jobify handle international tax documents and invoices?

Jobify automatically generates standard VAT/tax compliant digital invoices for every completed contract and milestone. You can download monthly and annual earnings summaries directly from your account settings for local tax filing.

What protection do I have if a client becomes unresponsive?

If you submit a milestone deliverable and the client does not respond within 14 calendar days, Jobify's automated escrow protection automatically approves and releases the escrowed funds to your wallet.
